# Ambience sounds

- `rain-on-window.mp3`: "Rain on Window" from the free ambient sound collection of Moodist
  (https://github.com/remvze/moodist). Moodist's sounds are under the Pixabay Content License or CC0,
  both free to use without attribution; credited here anyway. Cut to a 30-second seamless loop
  (crossfaded ends) and softened above 8 kHz with ffmpeg.
